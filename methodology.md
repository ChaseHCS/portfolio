# My methodology

> i am totally new to windows vuln research and the only reason im writing this is to serve as a foundational compass to start my learning.

From my limited understanding, at its core, everything in vulnerability research is really just the abstraction of three fundamental questions: **what does the program do, what inputs can be controlled by me, and how can the inputs manipulate the program.**

With this in mind we can come up with a more granular step-by-step process to conduct our research:

## 1. Info and knowledge gathering

This step includes:

1. Reading official docs
2. Reading past CVEs
3. Downloading and installing software
4. Reading community blogposts
5. Looking at similar products and viewing their CVEs
6. Documenting all relevant findings
